# KM4BE Spot Maps 0.1.0

A map of the stations on your app's spot board that you scroll and zoom, with a button to put one in your log.

## What it does

- **A map you scroll and zoom.** Drag the map east and west and it moves with your finger. To zoom, drag along the strip at the bottom of the map, or tap the plus and minus buttons. You can zoom in far enough to see a state or a country, on a tall phone or a wide one. When you are zoomed in, the map shows a band of latitudes and the up and down buttons move it north and south.
- **Regions that fill the screen.** The Region dropdown zooms to fit: North America, the Continental US, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia and Oceania each fill the pane, instead of leaving a continent small in one corner.
- **It opens where you are.** If your operation has a grid square, the map opens zoomed in on your own place, with you in the middle. If not, it shows the whole world.
- **Every spot the app has.** The stations come from the app's own spot board, the one the Spots panel shows, from every source you have set up: POTA, SOTA, WWFF and the rest. A station gets a dot if the app knows where its reference is, so a station at a park or a summit has a dot and a plain DX cluster spot does not. The line over the map says how many stations have no place. Two settings choose between everything on the board and only what the Spots panel is set to show, and how old the newest spots may be.
- **Tap a dot, then Use in the log.** Tapping a dot picks the station and says who it is, what it is on and how long ago it was spotted. Use in the log puts its call, frequency, band, mode and reference in the logging fields of the operation in front, and tunes your radio, just as a tap on a spot in the Spots panel does. A station you have already worked is drawn fainter.

## Good to know

- **The map fetches nothing itself.** The app tells it when its spot board changes. Two settings choose between everything on the board and only what the Spots panel shows, and how old the newest spots may be.
- **It asks for permission to control your radio**, because Use in the log tunes it. It does nothing with the radio otherwise.
