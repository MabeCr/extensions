**KM4BE Fuzzy Callsign Search** helps you spot a station you have already worked. Type part of a callsign in the call field, and this panel lists every station in the operation's log whose call contains it. For example, "be" finds KM4BE, W8ABE and VE3BEP.

## What it does

- **Partial matches.** The letters can be anywhere in the call: at the start, the middle or the end.
- **One line per station.** Each line shows the call with the matching letters in bold, then the band and mode, the date, and the name if you logged one.

## Good to know

- It searches only the operation that is open, not your other logs.
- It shows matches but doesn't fill in the call field for you. Keep typing to finish the call.
- Nothing leaves your device, and it asks for no permissions.
