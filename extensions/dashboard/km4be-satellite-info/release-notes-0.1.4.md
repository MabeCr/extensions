# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.1.4

Small update: the pane now tells you what kind of satellite you are looking at before you open it, and it is easier to use on a phone.

## What is new

- **Transponder type.** Each satellite in the pass list shows whether it is an FM repeater, an SSB/CW linear transponder, or a digital one (packet, telemetry, and other data modes). The Radio page of a pass shows the same.
- **Tap anywhere on a pass's row** to open it, not just on the blue arrow.
- **Favorites are remembered.** The satellites you have starred are still starred after the app is closed and opened again.

## Still true from 0.1.1

- It shows information only. It does not tune or control a radio yet.
- Not every satellite on the list has public orbit data, so some are listed with frequencies but no passes.

Feedback and corrections are welcome, especially satellite data that is out of date. Please reach out to KM4BE with suggestions!
