# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.6.0

The pass on a map of the world.

## New

- **Map tab.** A pass's page has a Map tab beside Sky. It shows the ground track, solid while the satellite is above your horizon and dashed for a quarter hour either side, green where it rises for you and red where it sets. It also shows where the satellite is right now, with the circle of ground it can be heard from, and where you are. Zoom and pan it as you like; it moves the satellite every couple of seconds while it is up.

## Good to know

- This version needs a Ham2K Logger that speaks extension API 25 or later, for the map.

## Still true from 0.5.0

- Doppler tracking keeps your radio on the downlink, and the uplink on a second receiver if you name one.
- Elevation and compass heading sit together in the sky chart's bottom left corner.
