# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.4.0

The pass list scrolls, and there is a new view of the sky with every satellite that is up right now.

## What is new

- **A list you scroll.** The list holds every pass of the next 24 hours, up to 110, and you scroll through it instead of paging five at a time. The chips and the UTC switch, the line about what is up and next, and the titles of the columns stay at the top, and the key to the report marks stays at the bottom, so you always know which column is which. Where you scrolled to is kept: when you come back from a pass's page the list is where you left it, and choosing All or Favorites starts again from the top.
- **Up Now.** A third choice next to All and Favorites. It draws the sky above you with every satellite that is above the horizon at this moment as a dot where it really is, its name beside it, and a ring round the ones you follow. Tap a dot to open that satellite's pass, and Back returns to the sky. The picture is redrawn every few seconds, so you can watch the satellites cross. When nothing is up, it says so and tells you which comes next.
- **The view you choose is remembered**, now including Up Now.
- **The marks column is titled Status**, which says more than Rpt did.

## Still true from 0.3.0

- It does not tune or control a radio yet.
- Not every satellite on the list has public orbit data, so some are listed with frequencies but no passes.
- Reports you send go to AMSAT's public status page, only when you confirm them.
