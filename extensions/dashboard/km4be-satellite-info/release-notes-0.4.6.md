# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.4.6

The sky chart now says which way to point, not just how high.

## New

- **Compass heading on the sky chart.** While a satellite is up, the bottom left corner of the chart shows its azimuth and point of the compass, like "Az 245° WSW", to go with the elevation written beside the satellite. It updates as the satellite moves.
- **The heading follows the scrub, too.** Drag a finger across the chart (or move the mouse over it) and the heading for that moment sits just above the time and elevation in the same corner, so you can plan where to aim before the pass starts.

## Still true from 0.4.5

- AMSAT's status marks and reports use status.amsat.org.
- The satellite list and the orbits are the app's offline data, and orbits come through Ham2K's caching proxy.
- It does not tune or control a radio yet.
