# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.1.5

Two changes to a pass's page.

## What is new

- **Run a finger along the pass.** On the Sky page, drag left to right anywhere over the plot (or move the mouse over it on a desktop) to step through the pass from rise to set. A marker follows the satellite's path, and the corners of the plot show the downlink and uplink frequencies with their Doppler shift at that moment, along with the time and elevation. It works for passes that have not started yet, so you can see the Doppler curve ahead of time. Lift your finger and the plot goes back to showing the live readout.
- **The sky plot keeps its size.** In a short pane the plot no longer shrinks to leave room for the lines of text under it; the text is cut off instead.

Feedback and corrections are welcome. Please reach out to KM4BE with suggestions!
