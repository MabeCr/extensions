# KM4BE Satellite Info 0.4.7

The numbers for where a satellite is now sit together in one place.

## Changed

- **Elevation and heading together.** While a satellite is up, the bottom left corner of the sky chart shows its elevation over its compass heading, like "El 45°" and "Az 245° WSW". The elevation is no longer written beside the satellite on its path, so the chart is the picture and the corner has the numbers.
- **Scrubbing reads the same way.** Drag a finger across the chart (or move the mouse over it) and the corner shows the time of that moment, then its elevation, then its heading.

## Still true from 0.4.6

- AMSAT's status marks and reports use status.amsat.org.
- The satellite list and the orbits are the app's offline data, and orbits come through Ham2K's caching proxy.
- It does not tune or control a radio yet.
