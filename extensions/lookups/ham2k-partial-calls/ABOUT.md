Know the call before you finish typing it. As you type a call, the calls it could be show under the call info, the likeliest first: the ones you have already worked in this contest, the ones you worked in it in past years, the ones anywhere in your logs, and the active contesters on [Super Check Partial](https://www.supercheckpartial.com). A call already in this operation's log is bold, a duplicate is red and struck through, and a new multiplier stands out. Each letter that differs from what you typed is marked in bold: green where the call adds one, red where it changes one, and a red bar where it leaves one out.

## Picking one

Tap a call to log it, or press **Alt-Y** to walk the list into the call field and **Shift-Alt-Y** to walk back. Walking past the last call brings back what you typed.

## The panel

For more than the line has room for, place the **Partial Call Checks** panel in your layout. It lists up to 30 calls, marked as the line marks them, in any operation, and a tap on one puts it in the call field, keeping what you have typed in the other fields; the list stays as it was until you type, so a wrong tap is put right with another. Its settings arrange the calls **By likelihood**, **By source** or **By match type**, and **Explain each** adds a line under each saying where it was found and whether it is a dupe or a new multiplier.

## Settings

In Settings, under Lookups, **Partial Calls Check** chooses when calls are offered and where they are looked for:

- **Only for Contests**: offers them in contest operations alone. Turn it off to have them in every operation.
- **Search your own logs**: every operation but this one, this contest's past years among them.
- **Search the current activity**: the calls worked in this operation.
- **Use Super Check Partial**: keeps W9KKN's list of calls active in contests on this device, about 8 MB, checked for updates once a day.

Super Check Partial is published by Mike Lyons, W9KKN, from the logs contesters submit.
